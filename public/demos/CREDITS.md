### Ember & Oak — shots considered and rejected

- `nJmTTcQrI2M` (chef handing a plate through a pass) — the page attributes it to
  **Getty Images** and offers no "Download free", so it is premium stock, not the
  Unsplash License. Rejected on licence.
- `Qv__CMihPUM` (cocktails on a floral bar table) — the page would not scrape, so
  its licence could not be confirmed. Rejected rather than assumed.
- `U1D6jokYALQ` (close-up steak on a grill) — carries the Unsplash+ lock.
- `r-_2WytZV-8` (warm booth interior, Mạnh Ngô) and `3QlfYs8VNc0` (gold-ceiling
  dining room, Martin Baron) — both free to use, but each has legible text inside
  the frame: a menu card and a foreign-language exit sign in the first, table
  tent cards reading "ELITE RESTAURANT" with Chinese characters in the second.
  A language on screen is a location signal, so neither ships. The same rule that
  retired the German-labelled VoltWise panel.
- `H0q4wRU_KmE` (amber pendant lamps over red booths, Karina G) — free to use, but
  a blurred figure stands behind the glass mid-frame. No identifiable face, yet a
  person is legible enough to be a guest at a table we never served, so it is out.
- `QfLfl7gjuUY` (loaf of bread in front of a fire) — licence line not present on
  the page, so unconfirmed.
- `Zh91T2Y9hfM` and `jQUmM7XC-Nw` — both free, but both turned out to be paper
  burning in a furnace rather than a kitchen oven, one of them with printed Chinese
  characters on the sheets. Wrong picture entirely.

## Notes

- No face is visible in any of these photos. The FlowRight hero shows hands and
  the back of a head only, so nobody in it can be identified. The VoltWise band
  shows a board and its wiring with no person in frame at all. Ember & Oak's two
  story photographs show hands only — `story-hands.webp` is cropped to forearms
  and a table, `story-dessert.webp` to hands and a bowl with a strand of hair at
  the very edge — so no guest or staff member can be recognised.
- Ember & Oak's gallery and hero contain no people at all.
- Maison Lune's hero, `stations.webp`, `corner.webp`, `tools.webp`, `towels.webp`
  and `marble.webp` contain no people at all. Its one person-led photograph,
  `braid.webp`, is a close crop of the back of a client's head — braids, an ear and
  a strand of neck hair — so no face is shown and nobody can be identified.
- The before/after drag slider originally planned for this demo was **dropped**:
  no genuine licence-verifiable pair of the same space was available, and a
  "before" was not to be faked with a filter, crop, or desaturated copy.
